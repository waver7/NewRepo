# 14. Advanced Version (the full vision, months 4–18+)

Everything in the MVP, plus the layers that make Lumen category-defining. Grouped by the
capability they unlock.

## 14.1 Complete Money Picture
- **PDF/OFX/QFX import** with balance-verified LLM extraction (doc 10) — the best statement
  importer on the market becomes a marketing claim.
- **Multi-aggregator** (MX/Finicity fallback; TrueLayer/GoCardless for EU/UK launch).
- **Investments & retirement:** holdings, allocation, contribution tracking, fee analysis
  ("your funds' expense ratios cost ~$430/yr").
- **Loans, mortgages, real assets** → full **net worth** with history and projections.
- **Multi-currency mastery:** unified home-currency view at historical rates, FX-fee
  detection, travel mode ("this trip cost $1,840 across 3 currencies and 4 cards").

## 14.2 Deep Intelligence
- **Cash-flow forecasting:** 30/60/90-day projected balance per account using recurring
  series + paycheck detection + seasonal spending baselines; shortfall windows flagged
  weeks ahead.
- **Anomaly engine v2** (doc 7 §7.5) with per-user threshold learning from feedback.
- **Item-level spending** via e-receipt email ingestion + receipt-photo OCR — category
  truth inside the basket ("groceries" minus the alcohol and household goods).
- **Refund/return matching** and dispute-worthy-charge surfacing.
- **"Needs vs wants" lens** and lifestyle-inflation tracking (spend growth vs income growth).
- **Benchmarks (opt-in, anonymized):** "households like yours spend ~$480/mo on groceries;
  you spend $610."

## 14.3 The AI Advisor (Premium flagship)
- Conversational interface over the user's own aggregates (tool-grounded, every number
  verifiable — doc 7 §7.6).
- Proactive briefs: monthly "CFO report" narrative; pre-purchase affordability checks
  ("Can I afford a $1,400 flight in May?" → forecast-based answer with trade-offs).
- Scenario planning: "What if rent goes to $1,800?" / "When could I afford a $60k car?"
- Annual **Year in Money** shareable review (organic-growth engine).

## 14.4 Action Layer (from insight → money moved)
This is the moat: closing the loop from *knowing* to *doing*.
- **Bill negotiation & cancellation concierge** (partner-powered; success-fee share).
- **Savings sweep:** real round-ups and rule-based transfers ("payday → move $200 to House
  Fund") via payment partner (Plaid Transfer/Dwolla/Astra) — added *only after* trust is
  established, with explicit per-rule consent and step-up auth.
- **HYSA marketplace:** quantified idle-cash offers ("$8,200 at 0.01% → ~$370/yr at 4.5%").
- **Card optimizer:** "given your actual spend mix, card X nets you +$240/yr" (affiliate,
  always net-benefit-ranked, methodology shown).
- **Credit score** monitoring (soft-pull partner) tied to debt payoff plans.

## 14.5 Households & Sharing
- **Household spaces:** partner sharing with per-account visibility controls (share the
  joint checking, keep the personal card private), shared budgets/goals, "who paid what"
  and settle-up math.
- Accountant/coach **read-only share links** (scoped, expiring).
- Data export API + Zapier/Sheets connectors for power users.

## 14.6 Platform Breadth
- **Web app** (read-heavy: big-screen reports, rules management, imports).
- Widgets, watch app (glance + voice quick-add), Siri/Assistant shortcuts.
- Localization: top 10 languages; region packs (date/number formats, local categories,
  local institutions, local subscription playbooks).

## 14.7 Premium Security Extras
- Passkey-only mode; travel mode (hide balances, decoy PIN); self-hosted export vault;
  SOC 2 Type II + annual public pentest summary as a trust page.

## 14.8 What We Still Say No To
- Selling data or attention. No ads, ever.
- Becoming a bank/issuing cards before the copilot wins on its own.
- Money movement without per-action consent and step-up auth.
- Crypto trading integration (track balances read-only at most).
